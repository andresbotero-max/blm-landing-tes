    A Colorful Guide to Our Feathered Friends

    🦅
    Bald Eagle
    Haliaeetus leucocephalus
    The majestic national bird of the United States, known for its striking white head and powerful build. These raptors are formidable hunters and skilled fishermen.
    Wingspan
    6-7 ft
    Weight
    10-14 lbs
    Lifespan
    20-30 yrs
    🐦
    Northern Cardinal
    Cardinalis cardinalis
    Vibrant red songbird beloved across eastern North America. Males sport brilliant crimson plumage while females wear warm brown tones with red highlights.
    Wingspan
    10-12 in
    Weight
    1.5 oz
    Lifespan
    3-15 yrs
    🦜
    Ruby-throated Hummingbird
    Archilochus colubris
    Tiny aerial acrobat with iridescent green plumage and a brilliant ruby-red throat. Can beat its wings up to 80 times per second and hover in mid-air.
    Wingspan
    3-4 in
    Weight
    0.1 oz
    Lifespan
    3-5 yrs
    🦉
    Great Horned Owl
    Bubo virginianus
    Powerful nocturnal predator with distinctive ear tufts and piercing yellow eyes. Known for its deep hooting call that echoes through the night.
    Wingspan
    3-5 ft
    Weight
    3 lbs
    Lifespan
    13-28 yrs
    🦆
    Mallard Duck
    Anas platyrhynchos
    Common and widespread dabbling duck. Males have distinctive iridescent green heads and yellow bills, while females sport mottled brown plumage.
    Wingspan
    32-39 in
    Weight
    2.4 lbs
    Lifespan
    5-10 yrs
    🕊️
    American Robin
    Turdus migratorius
    Beloved harbinger of spring with its cheerful song and orange breast. Often seen hopping on lawns hunting for earthworms and insects.
    Wingspan
    12-16 in
    Weight
    2.7 oz
    Lifespan
    2-6 yrs
    🐦
    Blue Jay
    Cyanocitta cristata
    Intelligent corvid with striking blue plumage and bold personality. Known for their loud calls and ability to mimic hawks and other birds.
    Wingspan
    13-17 in
    Weight
    2.5-3.5 oz
    Lifespan
    7-17 yrs
    🐤
    American Goldfinch
    Spinus tristis
    Often called "wild canaries" for their bright yellow breeding plumage. Strict vegetarians that perform acrobatic feeding displays on thistle plants.
    Wingspan
    7-9 in
    Weight
    0.4-0.7 oz
    Lifespan
    3-11 yrs
    🦆
    Wood Duck
    Aix sponsa
    Spectacularly colorful waterfowl. Males display iridescent plumage with intricate patterns that seem almost too elaborate to be real.
    Wingspan
    26-29 in
    Weight
    1.3 lbs
    Lifespan
    3-4 yrs

    🎯 Amazing Bird Facts!

    💨
    Peregrine falcons are the fastest animals on Earth, reaching speeds over 240 mph during hunting dives!
    🧠
    Crows and ravens can recognize individual human faces and hold grudges for years. They've been observed using tools and solving complex puzzles!
    🌍
    Arctic terns have the longest migration of any animal, traveling about 44,000 miles annually from Arctic to Antarctic and back!
    💪
    Owls can rotate their heads up to 270 degrees and have specialized feathers that allow completely silent flight!
    🎵
    Mockingbirds can learn and mimic over 200 different songs, including car alarms, cell phones, and other bird species!
    ❤️
    Penguins propose to their mates by presenting them with the smoothest pebble they can find. Many penguin species mate for life!
