Discover the Beauty of Our Feathered Friends

Explore the fascinating world of North American birds. From soaring eagles to tiny hummingbirds, each species tells a unique story of adaptation and survival.

🦅

Bald Eagle

Haliaeetus leucocephalus

The majestic national bird of the United States, known for its striking white head and powerful build. These raptors are formidable hunters and skilled fishermen.

Wingspan 6-7 ft
Weight 10-14 lbs
Lifespan 20-30 yrs
🦜

Northern Cardinal

Cardinalis cardinalis

Vibrant red songbird beloved across eastern North America. Males sport brilliant crimson plumage while females wear warm brown tones with red highlights.

Wingspan 10-12 in
Weight 1.5 oz
Lifespan 3-15 yrs
🐦

Ruby-throated Hummingbird

Archilochus colubris

Tiny aerial acrobat with iridescent green plumage and a brilliant ruby-red throat. Can beat its wings up to 80 times per second and hover in mid-air.

Wingspan 3-4 in
Weight 0.1 oz
Lifespan 3-5 yrs
🦉

Great Horned Owl

Bubo virginianus

Powerful nocturnal predator with distinctive ear tufts and piercing yellow eyes. Known for its deep hooting call that echoes through forests at night.

Wingspan 3-5 ft
Weight 3-4 lbs
Lifespan 13-28 yrs
🦆

Mallard Duck

Anas platyrhynchos

Common and widespread dabbling duck. Males feature glossy green heads and yellow bills, while females display mottled brown plumage perfect for camouflage.

Wingspan 32-37 in
Weight 2-3 lbs
Lifespan 5-10 yrs
🕊️

American Robin

Turdus migratorius

Beloved harbinger of spring with its cheerful song and orange-red breast. Often seen hopping across lawns in search of earthworms and insects.

Wingspan 12-16 in
Weight 2.7 oz
Lifespan 2-6 yrs
